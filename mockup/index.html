IIRegion XI

Five provinces around one gulf

Davao del Norte, Davao de Oro, Davao Oriental, Davao Occidental and Davao del Sur ring the Davao Gulf. This magazine pairs each province with one of the Indigenous peoples who live there.

The colours only tell the provinces apart. None are taken from any people’s designs.
